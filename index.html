Diabetes Mellitus & Advanced Glycemic Care

Comprehensive, personalized management for Type 1, Type 2, Gestational, and Secondary Diabetes with focus on organ protection and preventing complications.

MATERNAL & FETAL CARE

Pregnancy-Related Endocrine Disorders

Safeguarding maternal health and optimal fetal development through precision hormonal and glycemic control during pre-conception, pregnancy, and postpartum.

  • Gestational Diabetes Mellitus (GDM): Strict trimester-specific glycemic control (Fasting < 90 mg/dL, 1-hr postprandial < 140 mg/dL, 2-hr < 120 mg/dL) preventing fetal macrosomia, polyhydramnios, neonatal hypoglycemia, and emergency C-sections.
  • Pre-Existing Diabetes in Pregnancy: Pre-conception HbA1c optimization (target < 6.5%), teratogenic medication review, safe insulin transitions, and close maternal-fetal surveillance.
  • Thyroid Disorders in Pregnancy:
    • Maternal Hypothyroidism & Subclinical Hypothyroidism: Early TSH normalization to safeguard fetal neurological development and IQ potential.
    • Hyperthyroidism (Graves’ disease) in pregnancy: Safe trimester-based antithyroid drug selection (PTU vs. Methimazole) and fetal monitoring.
    • Postpartum Thyroiditis: Evaluation of transient thyrotoxicosis and subsequent hypothyroid phase post-delivery.
  • Pituitary & Prolactin Disorders: Pre-conception and antenatal management of micro- and macro-prolactinomas and visual field monitoring.
  • Gestational Endocrine Hypertension: Screening for underlying endocrine hypertension and pre-eclampsia metabolic risk factors.

Pediatric Growth Problems & Puberty Disorders

Specialized pediatric endocrine evaluation to ensure every child reaches their genetic height potential, addressing short stature, growth faltering, and pubertal timing.

  • Short Stature Evaluation: Accurate height stadiometry, growth velocity charting, bone age assessment (Greulich-Pyle), and comparison against mid-parental target height.
  • Growth Hormone Deficiency (GHD): Dynamic growth hormone stimulation testing (Clonidine / Glucagon provocative tests), serum IGF-1 / IGFBP-3 profiling, and recombinant Human Growth Hormone (rhGH) therapy with regular monitoring.
  • Constitutional Delay of Growth and Puberty (CDGP): Differentiating physiological late bloomers from pathological growth disorders.
  • Precocious (Early) Puberty: Diagnostic evaluation for breast budding or testicular enlargement before age 8 in girls and age 9 in boys; GnRH analog therapy to prevent premature epiphyseal plate fusion.
  • Delayed Puberty: Systematic workup for hypogonadotropic hypogonadism, hypergonadotropic hypogonadism, or constitutional delays in adolescents.
  • Genetic & Chromosomal Growth Syndromes: Turner Syndrome, Noonan Syndrome, and Small for Gestational Age (SGA) lacking catch-up growth.
  • Monthly Growth Clinic: Held on the First Saturday of every month for dedicated pediatric anthropometric and hormonal assessment.

PATIENT EDUCATION & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions regarding endocrinology services, diagnostic preparations, and specialized treatments.

An endocrinologist is a super-specialist medical doctor who diagnoses and treats complex disorders of the endocrine glands and hormones. You should consult an endocrinologist for uncontrolled diabetes, gestational diabetes, thyroid disorders, abnormal weight gain or obesity, pediatric short stature, irregular menses or PCOS, osteoporosis, recurrent kidney stones, or pituitary/adrenal gland issues.

Elevated blood sugar during pregnancy (Gestational Diabetes Mellitus - GDM) crosses the placenta directly to the growing baby, forcing the fetal pancreas to overproduce insulin. This can lead to fetal macrosomia (abnormally large baby), birth trauma, neonatal hypoglycemia, respiratory distress, and long-term metabolic risks. Under Dr. Nagapriya’s protocols, strict trimester-specific glucose targets safeguard both maternal health and safe fetal delivery.

Parents should seek evaluation if a child is noticeably shorter than classmates of the same age and gender, is growing less than 5 cm per year, appears much shorter than expected from parents' heights, or experiences unusually early (before age 8 in girls, age 9 in boys) or delayed puberty. The Monthly Growth Clinic (held on the 1st Saturday of every month) specializes in bone age X-rays, Growth Hormone testing, and timely intervention before bone growth plates fuse.

Ordinary dieting often fails because biological hunger hormones (such as ghrelin and leptin resistance) aggressively force the body to regain lost weight. Medical obesity management at SPS Hospital treats weight as a hormonal disease: screening for endocrine abnormalities, using clinically approved modern incretin pharmacotherapies (GLP-1 and GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonists) under supervision, and tailoring metabolic nutrition to preserve muscle mass while burning visceral fat.

For Fasting Blood Glucose, Lipid Profile, Fasting Insulin, and morning Cortisol, a 10 to 12-hour overnight fast is recommended (water is permitted). Thyroid profiles (FT3, FT4, TSH) generally do not require strict fasting, but morning samples are best. If you take thyroid medication (Levothyroxine), take your blood test before consuming your morning tablet, unless specifically instructed otherwise by Dr. Nagapriya.
