## 🩺 Comprehensive Scope of Endocrinology Services

### 1. Diabetes Mellitus & Advanced Glycemic Care (Highlight Service)
- Precision management for Type 1, Type 2, Secondary, and Gestational Diabetes.
- Cardio-renal-metabolic organ preservation using SGLT-2i and GLP-1 RA.
- Continuous Glucose Monitoring (CGM) and Ambulatory Glucose Profile (AGP) tracking.
- Proactive screening for Diabetic Retinopathy, Nephropathy, Peripheral Neuropathy, and Diabetic Foot Ulcers.

### 2. Pregnancy-Related Endocrine Disorders (Highlight Service)
- Strict trimester-specific glycemic control for Gestational Diabetes Mellitus (GDM) (Fasting < 90 mg/dL, 1-hr post-meal < 140 mg/dL, 2-hr < 120 mg/dL).
- Fetal neurocognitive protection through early maternal thyroid optimization (Hypothyroidism / Hashimoto's / Hyperthyroidism in pregnancy).
- Antenatal prolactinoma and endocrine-associated hypertension management.

### 3. Pediatric Growth Problems & Pubertal Disorders (Highlight Service)
- Short stature evaluation vs. mid-parental genetic target height.
- Growth Hormone Deficiency (GHD) diagnostics, Clonidine / Glucagon GH stimulation tests, and recombinant Human Growth Hormone (rhGH) therapy.
- Precocious (early) puberty and delayed puberty evaluation.
- **Monthly Growth Clinic:** Conducted on the **First Saturday of every month**.

### 4. Comprehensive Obesity & Medical Weight Management (Highlight Service)
- Treating obesity scientifically as a complex neuroendocrine and metabolic disease.
- Comprehensive screening for secondary hormonal causes (Cushing's, hypothyroidism, insulin resistance).
- Modern evidence-based incretin therapies (GLP-1 receptor agonists and dual GIP/GLP-1 agonists).
- Visceral adiposity reset, fatty liver (MASLD) management, and pre/post-bariatric surgery care.
- **Monthly Obesity Clinic:** Conducted on the **Fourth Saturday of every month**.

### 5. Thyroid & Parathyroid Disorders
- Hypothyroidism, Graves' Disease, Toxic Multinodular Goitre, Subacute Thyroiditis.
- High-resolution Thyroid Ultrasound, TIRADS stratification, and USG-guided FNAC.
- Post-thyroidectomy cancer surveillance and serum thyroglobulin monitoring.
- Hyperparathyroidism and disorders of calcium metabolism.

### 6. Osteoporosis & Metabolic Bone Diseases
- Post-menopausal and senile osteoporosis assessment (FRAX, DEXA scans).
- Anabolic bone building (Teriparatide) and anti-resorptive therapies (Bisphosphonates, Denosumab).
- Vitamin D deficiency, adult osteomalacia, and pediatric rickets.

### 7. PCOS & Reproductive Endocrinology
- Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS / PCOD): irregular cycles, hirsutism, acne, metabolic syndrome.
- Endocrine factors in male and female infertility.
- Male hypogonadism (testosterone deficiency) and gynecomastia.

### 8. Pituitary & Adrenal Gland Disorders
- Pituitary adenomas: Prolactinomas, Acromegaly, Cushing's disease, hypopituitarism.
- Adrenal insufficiency (Addison's disease), Congenital Adrenal Hyperplasia (CAH).
- Endocrine hypertension workup (Conn's syndrome, Pheochromocytoma).
